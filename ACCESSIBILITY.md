# Accessibility

I am legally blind, and I need tools that let me do what I need to do with the same ease as before. Accessibility and ease of use are the motivation for LazyVM.

## Priorities

LazyVM exists to make tools that are not very accessible usable by everyone.

## Contributor expectations

- Learn from real users. There are many excellent resources: watch people who use assistive technology review products, give tutorials and demonstrate different use cases.
- Check that your change works with a screen reader and by keyboard alone.

## Reporting accessibility issues

For now, [open an accessibility issue](https://github.com/downerboy/LazyVM/issues/new?template=accessibility.yml).

### Severity

Every accessibility issue gets the highest severity. A user must be able to use this product without exerting more effort than any other user. As a user myself, I find it disrespectful to report a problem and never receive a response or see it addressed.

### How we respond

An accessibility issue is addressed first, before any other work.

## Ownership and maintenance

I use a screen reader just to develop this project, so I feel these problems every day.

## Supported environments

LazyVM supports Windows.

## Known limitations

I cannot know the limitations unless they are brought to my attention. Everyone has different limitations, so please tell me about yours.
