# Code of Conduct

LazyVM is built by and for people who need tools that work for them, including people who use a screen reader. Everyone who takes part here is expected to help keep it a respectful, welcoming place.

## What is expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are learning.
- Assume good faith, and say plainly when something does not work for you.
- Describe problems in words, not only in screenshots, so everyone can read them.
- Accept feedback on your work gracefully, and give it about the work, not the person.

## What is not accepted

- Harassment, insults, threats or demeaning comments, in any form.
- Discrimination, including about disability or the way someone uses a computer.
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues or pull requests.

## Scope

This applies to the repository's issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space where someone represents the project.

## Reporting

Report a problem by messaging the maintainer, [@downerboy](https://github.com/downerboy), on GitHub, or privately through the **Security** tab if it is sensitive. Reports are kept confidential.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests, and may warn, temporarily block or permanently block anyone whose behavior breaks this code. The maintainer's decision is final.

This code follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
